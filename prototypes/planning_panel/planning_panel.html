Planning panel, as built

A working copy of the in-game planning panel (turn 1, test fleet). Click cards, press Movement and plot on the map, order sail, submit, end planning. Notes B1–B30 below describe the current behaviour so comments can point at them.

Changes so far

  1. C1Ship cards drop the hull, rigging, sail and crew quality/morale lines (B3). Hull and rigging stay in the crew block and the sail in the Sail block, for the selected ship only.
  2. C2Ship cards are an accordion. The selected card is expanded: title row, chips, Movement / Crew & damage / Status, then that ship’s Hull, Rigging and Guns box. Every other card collapses to one row: health icon (hull and rigging bars, amber under two thirds, red under one third; red border when fouled, orange when afire), name, badge, speed, ✓ once orders are in. Selecting a card collapses the others. The box stays visible while plotting. DAMAGE (and the roster while the overlay is open) stays below the cards for the selected ship.
  3. C3The expanded card’s box adds a Crew line under Rigging (a segment per box, grouped by section S1–S4, each labelled with its station: guns, or a task in amber, fire in orange; caption gives crew quality and morale; see C4) and a Marines line under it (marine boxes, caption gives their station), both above Guns. Every ship gets them when its card is expanded.
  4. C4The Crew caption names the crew quality instead of its letter (A Prime Seaman, B Able Seaman, C Ordinary Seaman, D Landsman, E Waister, F Green Hand, G Lubber, the names the map’s status bars already use); the letter is in its tooltip.
  5. C5What the marines are doing (“muskets at the rail”) moves from the line under Guns to the Marines line caption; the Guns readout keeps only the sections line and why sections are missing.
  6. C6Guns becomes a line under Marines, like the others. Caption: sections firing and their modifier (red when −6 or worse). Two rows, Port and Starboard, a segment per gun factor (light: long guns, dark: carronades; the game counts guns as factors per side, not single guns). Dismounted guns show as empty segments from the end of that side’s row, with “n dismounted · −n” in amber; otherwise “ready”. Under the rows, why any sections are missing.
  7. C7The DAMAGE (n) list leaves the side panel and sits at the bottom of the Crew & damage overlay for the selected ship (fires, critical hits, repairable boxes; “No damage.” when there is none). Below the cards the side panel now shows only the section roster, while the overlay is open.
  8. C8The section roster moves into the Crew & damage overlay too, above the DAMAGE list. The side panel goes straight from the ship cards to the rule and the Sail block; the selected card still hides its chips while the overlay is open.

Current behaviour

Layout

  1. B1The panel is the left context panel, 460 px wide at 1080 px tall, titled “Planning Phase”, collapsible with <<. Inside: “Your Fleet”, a scrolling area, then the instruction line and End Planning pinned to the bottom.
  2. B2The scroll area holds, in order: one card per ship, the crew block (selected ship only; C2: now only DAMAGE and the roster, the rest moved into the card), a rule, the Sail block (selected ship only), the Movement plotting block (only while plotting).
  3. B3A card: icon, “Name — type”, a red badge (FOULED, GRAPPLED, DISMASTED, STRUCK, PRIZE, DESTROYED, plus AFIRE / AFIRE ×n in orange and BOARDERS), “Speed n” (last turn’s speed), the crew summary chips, then the buttons. C2: only the selected card shows all of this; the others collapse to a row (see C2). Changed (C1): the game also shows four detail lines here (hull and rigging boxes per section, sail by name, crew quality and morale); they are removed in this prototype.
  4. B4C2: buttons appear only on the expanded card, so another ship must be selected before its Movement can be pressed. In the game: card buttons: Movement and Status on every card; “Crew & damage (C)” only on the selected card. The Crew and Maintenance buttons in the scene are hidden.
  5. B5Card colours: grey normal, blue-grey with a thicker border when selected, green once its orders are in. Selected wins over submitted. “(orders given)” is added to the name.
  6. B6Chips: Auto (blue) when crew allocation is automatic; one chip per group of sections on the same station (“S1 S2 S3 S4 guns”, “M fire” muted, tasks plain, fire parties orange); unfought fires and plan warnings as red outlined chips. The selected card hides its chips while the crew overlay is open.
  7. B7C2: bars and GUNS now sit in the expanded card. In the game, the crew block: hull and rigging bars (a segment per box, grouped by section, an emptied section outlined red; rigging caption says the most sail it can carry), GUNS readout (“n of 4 sections · modifier”, why sections are missing, what the marines do), a collapsible DAMAGE (n) list. While the overlay is open it adds the roster of sections.

Selection and plotting

  1. B8The first ship is selected when planning opens. Clicking a card or the ship on the map selects it; the map pans only if the ship is near the edge.
  2. B9Selecting another ship cancels any plot in progress for the previous one, without asking.
  3. B10Movement is a toggle. Pressing it selects that ship, closes the crew overlay and starts a plot. Pressing it again (or Cancel) ends the plot. Only one ship plots at a time.
  4. B11While plotting, the crew block is hidden to make room; the Sail block stays and the Movement plotting block appears below it. The panel layout jumps when this happens. C2: the card’s Hull, Rigging and Guns box stays; only DAMAGE hides.
  5. B12Plotting block: “Movement: n of m left” (m fixed for the plot), notes in pale yellow (allowance changed on a new heading, head to wind / in irons, limited by acceleration), orange collision warning (on hover), “Path:” as axial coordinates, tacking and steering notes in amber, then Undo, Undo All, Submit, Cancel.
  6. B13Submit reads “Submit (hold position)” when the path is empty and staying put is allowed. Undo and Undo All are disabled with no steps.
  7. B14Submitting marks the card green, draws the submitted path on the map and hides the plotting block. The ship is now counted toward End Planning.
  8. B15Pressing Movement on a ship that already has orders withdraws them (card back to grey, path cleared, count goes down) and starts a fresh plot. Its sail order is kept.
  9. B16A ship that cannot move (fouled, grappled, dismasted, struck, prize) has Movement disabled with the reason as a tooltip and counts as having orders from the start.

Sail

  1. B17The Sail block shows “Now: <sail>”, then one of: “No change ordered. Choose one, or keep this sail.” (amber) with a button per reachable sail and its cost in turns and sections; “Ordered: X. Press it again to cancel.” with the ordered button marked ●; or “Changing to X — n turns left” with no buttons while a change is under way.
  2. B18Each sail press is a server request; a refusal shows in red under the buttons. Sail can be ordered with or without a movement plot, and is not part of Submit.
  3. B19The sail hint “Sections changing sail cannot serve the guns.” is always shown. The crew chips and GUNS readout change as soon as a change is ordered.
  4. B20The Sail block is hidden for ships out of play, and when nothing can be ordered.

Turn flow

  1. B21The instruction line: “n ships still need orders. Select each and press Movement.”, then “All ships have orders. Press End Planning.” (or “No ship can move this turn…”).
  2. B22End Planning (n/m) is disabled until every ship has submitted. Holding position still needs Movement, then Submit.
  3. B23End Planning sends the whole plan. Crew plots are then made legal against the movement plot; the panel is replaced by movement playback.
  4. B24Crew & damage (or C) opens the crew overlay over the map for the selected ship; Esc closes it; plotting a move closes it; it closes when planning ends.
  5. B25Status opens the separate ship status panel for that ship.

Not reproduced here

  1. B26The map is schematic: no wind-based allowance changes beyond head to wind, no consecutive-turn limits, no tacking roll at MA 0, no blocked wind. The panel’s responses are the same shape as the game’s.
  2. B27The crew overlay and status panel are placeholders; drag-and-drop crew assignment is out of scope for this panel.
  3. B28Sail costs, crew allocation and damage numbers are sample data, not read from the rules tables.
  4. B29Bear-off, fire declaration and maintenance prompts are other panels and are not shown.
